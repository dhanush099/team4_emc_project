OUR STORY

Built by travellers,
for travellers.

Wanderly began in 2026 with a small team that was tired of confusing itineraries and surprise charges. We decided to build the agency we wished we had: friendly, transparent, and focused on the traveller. What started as weekend getaways for friends and family has grown into a full travel agency. Today we work with trusted hotels, guides, and local partners across India and abroad, and we still plan every trip with the same care as our first one.

What we stand for

🎯

Our Mission

To take the stress out of planning. We handle bookings, transport, and schedules, and we price every package honestly. Whether it is a weekend break or a two-week tour, you always know what you are paying for and what to expect.

🔭

Our Vision

To become the most trusted travel agency for first-time and frequent travellers alike. We want to be known for fair deals, local expertise, and care for the places we visit, so that travel benefits both the visitor and the community.

🤝

Our Promise

A real person is always available to help, before you book, during your trip, and after you return. If a flight changes or a plan falls through, our team steps in quickly with a solution, so you never feel stranded.
